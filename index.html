Gap-time principles

A useful slot should fit your actual energy and surroundings.

10–20 min
Quick recall

Flashcards, formula review, or summarising one concept.

20–40 min
Focused practice

Three problems, a short quiz, or one small coding task.

40+ min
Deep work

Past-paper questions, a programming exercise, or structured revision.

Low energy
Recovery counts

Walk, hydrate, eat, or reset before the next class.
